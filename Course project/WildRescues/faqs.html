Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I find an injured animal?

Call us immediately at (814) 555-8989. Do not try to feed the animal or give it water.

Can I volunteer?

Yes! Volunteers help with feeding, cleaning, fundraising, and more. Contact us for details.

Do you accept donations?

We rely on donations to fund food, medical care, and supplies for rescued wildlife.

Are you a nonprofit?

Yes, Wild Rescues is a registered nonprofit organization.

Do you offer educational programs?

We host educational events for schools, groups, and community organizations.
